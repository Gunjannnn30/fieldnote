How does this work? (for the curious)

Three searchers, one question. When you ask something, Fieldnote searches the manuals and transcripts (as words), the pictures (as pixels, not as descriptions) and the recorded calls (as sound) at the same time. A typed question reaches the picture searcher because the same model that reads pictures also understands text.

Why not just describe the pictures? Because a description of a pressure graph says "a line going up" — and the number the technician needs, 385, is not in it. The number is destroyed when the picture is described, long before anyone asks a question. Here the picture stays a picture until the answer has been read out of it.

What "lines read" means. The answer is not a whole page. It is one line - a sentence from a manual, a label on a chart, a line from a phone call. Those lines are pulled out of the best-matching pages, re-ranked, and the winner becomes the answer.

Why there is a picture next to the answer. So you do not have to take the system's word for it. The crop is the exact region the answer was read from; "full page" shows you where that region sits on the page.
